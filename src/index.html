Keys
'h' - Toggle this panel
'0' - Toggle 1/4 views
'1' - Front view
'2' - Left view
'3' - Top view
'4' - 4th view
'8' - Toggle 4th view (Oblique vs Axonometric)
'9' - Parallel vs Perspective

'e' - Move backward
'w' - Raise cannon
's' - Lower cannon
'a' - Rotate cabin ccw
'd' - Rotate cabin cw
'z' - Shoot a tomato
'f' - Fire the missiles
'g' - Reload the missiles
'r' - Reset view params
'←' - Increase theta
'→' - Decrease theta
'↑' - Increase gamma
'↓' - Decrease gamma
